Findings

  • pair_01 — Physical vs digital product critical
    Only product_type changes: physical → digital; other facts held constant (40.00 USD, tracking_available=false). Shipment tracking is a physical-goods concept; a digital or service claim cannot be judged by tracking, so the decision must depend on product type. Under the candidate policy the base (product_type=physical) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD] and the twin (product_type=digital) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD]. Unintended policy expansion: the twin (product_type=digital) received the same AUTO_REFUND outcome as the base (product_type=physical).
  • pair_03 — USD vs EUR medium
    Only currency changes: USD → EUR; other facts held constant (40.00 USD, tracking_available=false). The threshold is denominated in one currency. Without a conversion rule, the same number in another currency is a different amount of money. Under the candidate policy the base (currency=USD) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD] and the twin (currency=EUR) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD]. Ambiguous behavior (CURRENCY_CONVERSION_UNDEFINED): needs human evaluation.
  • pair_04 — First refund request vs retried request critical
    Only request_attempts changes: 1 → 2; other facts held constant (40.00 USD, tracking_available=false). A retried request for the same claim must never pay out twice. The decision should stay the same and the money effects must not double. Under the candidate policy the base (request_attempts=1) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD] and the twin (request_attempts=2) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD]. Financial invariant failed: ONE_REFUND_PER_LOGICAL_REQUEST. Invariant evidence: ONE_REFUND_PER_LOGICAL_REQUEST — logical request claim-run_90284b3e-scenario_08 produced 2 successful refunds (REF-SIM-00023, REF-SIM-00024).
  • pair_05 — No dispute vs open dispute critical
    Only dispute_state changes: none → open; other facts held constant (40.00 USD, tracking_available=false). Refunding while a dispute is open risks returning the same money twice, so an open dispute must change the route. Under the candidate policy the base (dispute_state=none) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD] and the twin (dispute_state=open) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD]. Unintended policy expansion: the twin (dispute_state=open) received the same AUTO_REFUND outcome as the base (dispute_state=none). Invariant evidence: NO_AUTO_REFUND_WITH_OPEN_DISPUTE — 1 automatic refund(s) issued while a dispute was open (double-recovery risk).
  • pair_06 — Normal webhook vs duplicate webhook critical
    Only webhook_deliveries changes: 1 → 2; other facts held constant (40.00 USD, tracking_available=false). PayPal can redeliver webhooks. A duplicate delivery must not create a duplicate operation or move local state backwards. Under the candidate policy the base (webhook_deliveries=1) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD] and the twin (webhook_deliveries=2) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD]. Financial invariant failed: ONE_REFUND_PER_LOGICAL_REQUEST, DUPLICATE_WEBHOOK_NO_DUPLICATE_OPERATION, OLDER_WEBHOOK_NO_STATE_REGRESSION. Invariant evidence: ONE_REFUND_PER_LOGICAL_REQUEST — logical request claim-run_90284b3e-scenario_12 produced 2 successful refunds (REF-SIM-00036, REF-SIM-00037); DUPLICATE_WEBHOOK_NO_DUPLICATE_OPERATION — event WH-run_90284b3e-scenario_12-claim triggered 2 operations across duplicate deliveries; OLDER_WEBHOOK_NO_STATE_REGRESSION — local state moved backwards after event WH-run_90284b3e-scenario_12-captured (to COMPLETED).
  • pair_08 — Digital: evidence missing vs confirms non-delivery high
    Only evidence_status changes: missing → confirms_non_delivery; other facts held constant (40.00 USD, tracking_available=false). For goods without shipment tracking, the decision must hinge on delivery/access evidence: confirmed non-delivery and missing evidence are not the same case. Under the candidate policy the base (evidence_status=missing) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD] and the twin (evidence_status=confirms_non_delivery) → AUTO_REFUND [TRACKING_UNAVAILABLE, WITHIN_THRESHOLD]. Unintended policy expansion: the twin (evidence_status=confirms_non_delivery) received the same AUTO_REFUND outcome as the base (evidence_status=missing).

Recommended policy correction

Proposed by the Policy Repair agent (deterministic). A human must approve it; it is then re-tested against every scenario.

Proposed (Version B)

Automatically refund transactions of $50 or less when tracking information is unavailable.

Corrected (Version C)

For physical goods of $50 or less, automatically refund a missing-delivery claim when valid shipment tracking is unavailable. For digital goods, require digital-delivery or access evidence. If the evidence is missing or conflicting, send the case for review. Apply the same evidence requirement to services. Only USD transactions are in scope; transactions in any other currency go to review until a conversion rule is approved. If a dispute is open on the transaction, send the case for review. Treat retried refund requests as the same logical request using a stable PayPal-Request-Id, and ignore duplicate webhook deliveries using event-ID deduplication.
